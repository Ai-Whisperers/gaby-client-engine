## A. Five "play patterns" dentists have used to grow

Every successful dentist-style clinic growth story I've seen falls into one of these 5 patterns:

### Pattern 1 - "Specialist moat" (one thing nobody else does)
A dentist becomes THE local expert in 1 procedure, writes about it, and referrals follow. Examples documented:
- **Invisalign Diamond Provider status** - dentists pay the platform, "Diamond Provider" badge drives inbound
- **Slow Dentistry / Endodontists / Prosthodontists** - formal specialist credentials drive referrals from generalists
- **Malo All-on-4 clinics** (Lisbon, Bangkok) - flagship implant-technique specialty
- **Picasso Dental Vietnam** - 70,000 patients from 62 countries via remote second-opinion specialty

### Pattern 2 - "Brand human" (the dentist IS the brand)
The dentist's personality is the marketing. Examples:
- **Dr. Mike Varshavski** - 25M followers via being human first ("doctor's are awkward on social media" is the objection he breaks)
- **Dr. Mike Jin (Pasadena)** - founded 2021, brand = full-mouth aesthetic, IG-first
- **Dr. Mike Dostal / Mill Creek Dental (KC)** - smile-giveaway + IG Reels
- **Smiles by Dr. Michael Allen** - startup to $100-110K monthly in 13 months, 8 operatories

### Pattern 3 - "Referral network architect" (B2B relationships)
The dentist cultivates 30+ professionals who refer. Examples documented:
- **Pearl AI case study**: practices with formal referral agreements see 30-50% of new patients via word-of-mouth
- **Dr. Benjamin Turnwald**: edge = treating patients like a team
- **Thailand second-opinion specialists** (BIDC, Bangkok Smile, Thantakit) - 30%+ of cases through formal B2B relationships with embassies, concierge desks, hotels

### Pattern 4 - "Educational publisher" (free content as funnel)
Dentist publishes educational content -> trust -> patient acquisition. Examples:
- **Zack Chug (3K->200K IG in 9 months)** via saving high-value posts algorithm rewards
- **Picasso Dental Vietnam** does free remote second-opinion consults - 65% convert to booked treatment (documented)
- **Thailand second-opinion packages** explicitly market "remote consult before flying"

### Pattern 5 - "Insurance/payer arbitrage" (B2B contracts)
The dentist builds contracts with employers, insurers, benefit programs. Examples:
- **ROOT Periodontics** case study: 29% new patient increase via care-stack platform
- **Paraguay analog**: Sanatorio Migone, Hospital Britanico
- **Lab commission kickbacks** (5-10% per case) - common across LATAM and US

# 8 New Ideas from Dentists Around the World

These are growth tactics observed in actual dentists' growth stories. **None are in the strategy repo's 30+32 idea catalogue** because they came from outside the team.

## 1. Real-time second-opinion via WhatsApp

**Pattern source:** Picasso Dental Vietnam — 6 clinics, 70,000 patients from 62 countries. Free remote second-opinion consults via WhatsApp. Approximately **65% of those who complete a remote consult go on to book treatment**.

**What they do:**

1. Patient sends panoramic X-ray (OPG) + photos + medical history via WhatsApp
2. Within 48 hours they receive a detailed treatment plan with:
   - Procedure-by-procedure rationale
   - Fixed USD pricing (no hidden fees)
   - Realistic timeline
   - Alternative options
3. ~85–95% diagnostic concordance with in-person assessment (per teledentistry literature)

**Why this fits Gaby:** Second-opinion escrita is already her differentiator. Moving it from "in-person" to "in-person OR remote WhatsApp" is one SOP change. Removes the geographic fence for expats + interior PY + busy Asunción residents.

**How to implement (1 week):**

1. Write 1 WhatsApp auto-reply template: "Gracias por tu mensaje. Para una segunda opinión, envíame por favor: (a) radiografía panorámica, (b) 3 fotos de la zona a tratar, (c) la propuesta de plan que te dieron."
2. Add a Google Form for X-ray upload (HIPAA-style precautions via encrypted link).
3. Block 30 minutes/day for reviewing submissions.
4. Price the deliverable: Gs 800k for written second opinion, with a 30-min call slot to walk through it.

**Effort:** 1 week setup. **Expected:** 3–10 remote second-opinion requests per month, 65% conversion = 2–7 new patient starts/month.

## 2. "The Ethical Question" micro-course for recién recibidos

**Pattern source:** The mercado-recien-recibidos research in `dentist/01_RESEARCH/market/mercado-recién-recibidos-y-vacío-mentoría-2026.md` documents that **NO structured dental coaching exists in Paraguay today** for the ~1,200–2,100 dentists in the 0–3 year post-reception cohort.

**What's interesting:** Most "dental coaching" online is US-focused with US regulations. PY-specific could be a category-creator.

**What they could do:**

1. 6 modules × 30 minutes each, on WhatsApp-recorded voice notes (no video needed — Gaby doesn't need to be on camera):
   - Module 1: "Cómo decir NO a un tratamiento que no es necesario"
   - Module 2: "Cuándo hablar de precio antes de hacer el plan"
   - Module 3: "Cómo decirle a un colega que no estás de acuerdo"
   - Module 4: "El protocolo de consentimiento informado"
   - Module 5: "Cómo cobrar sin pelearte"
   - Module 6: "El día que sale mal — qué hacer"

2. Deliver via WhatsApp Business broadcast list to the ~30 named dentists from `dentist/03_LAUNCH/referral-program/01-list-target-colleagues-asuncion.md`.
3. Charge Gs 300k total per dentist (one-time). 6 months of weekly voice notes.

**Why this fits Gaby:** Gaby already has 20+ years of ethical decision-making logged in her own practice. The "ethical dilemma" angle matches her "conservadora + Te escucho" brand. **It's defensible: every other course on the market is about technique. Nobody owns this niche.**

**Effort:** 4h to record first 1 module. **Expected:** 10–30 dentists enroll = Gs 3-9M in year 1.

## 3. Smile Giveaway — community votes on a free full case

**Pattern source:** Dr. Mike Dostal (Mill Creek Dental, Kansas City). Posts 5 finalist stories on Instagram + Facebook, community votes via like/share/comment, winner gets free full-arch restoration (~$15-25k value). Publicity = thousands of impressions per finalist.

**Why this fits Gaby:**

- Gaby's conservador brand + second-opinion reputation = no danger of looking "Disney/DIWhy clickbait"
- The narrative ("community picks") generates massive free PR
- The case becomes a year-long case study (story → weekly IG posts for 6+ months)

**Why I'm cautious:**

- Cost: 1 case = Gs 15-25M (single full-arch)
- Brand risk if winner's outcome is subpar
- Requires a long-form story apparatus (filming, patient testimonials, consent)

**Effort:** 6 months lead time. **Expected if executed:** 100-300 patient inquiries over the campaign, 30+ in the case-study folder for future content.

**Verdict:** Skip for Y1. **Reconsider at month 6** if the rest of the playbook is delivering patients.

## 4. Bilingual clinical case publication (ES + EN)

**Pattern source:** Synthesized from observation of how expat-dentist markets work (e.g., dental tourism clinics in Mexico, Costa Rica, Thailand) — they publish case studies in **both their local language and English** because the English version ranks for expat searches while the local version ranks locally.

**What they do:**

Every before/after case becomes:
- 1 Spanish blog post (ranked for `rehabilitación oral Asunción`)
- 1 English blog post (ranked for `oral rehabilitation Paraguay`)
- 1 IG reel (ES)
- 1 IG reel (EN with subtitles or same reel)
- 1 newsletter send (both lists)

**Why this fits Gaby:**

- Gaby's English fluency is already in the strategy documents (`expat-landing-page-content.md` is already written)
- The bilingual reach is double the addressable market in Asunción alone
- Existing blog infrastructure exists at `/en/segunda-opinion` and `/es/segunda-opinion`

**Effort:** 1.5× the writer cost per case (2 versions). **Expected:** 2× the organic SEO traffic from case studies over 6 months.

## 5. "The Gaby File" — quarterly private newsletter

**Pattern source:** Standard publisher-pattern newsletter (Substack, Morning Brew, Lenny's Newsletter). Captures emails for a quarterly send.

**What's interesting:** A quarterly newsletter (not weekly) has super-low effort (1 send/3 months) but compounds the brand over years. The 4 issues a year are: pre-apertura, summer, year-end, January.

**What goes in each:**

- 1 case study from the quarter
- 1 short answer to a question Gaby got asked
- 1 dental myth Gaby wants to bust
- 1 announcement of next-month event or service

**Effort:** 2h/issue. **Expected:** email-list growth of ~50/newsletter issue from existing traffic; once list is >500, ad/sponsor revenue becomes possible.

## 6. Reciprocal-display racks at partner clinics

**Pattern source:** Common in aesthetic dentistry across LATAM and US — partner clinics display each other's brochures / business cards. Often unwritten and overlooked.

**What Gaby does:**

1. Print 100 small (10×15 cm) branded cards with "Segundas opiniones + rehabilitación oral — Dra. González Pane, +595 987 126 790" + the אומץ logo.
2. Drop 30 cards at 3-5 spa partners (Spa Tiger, Beauty & Spa Body Line, Teranu, etc. — top-priority spa rows in the outreach pack).
3. Each spa partner agrees to display a stack of Gaby's cards on their counter; Gaby displays their brochure on her counter.

**Effort:** Print cost (Gs 200k for 100 cards) + 4 partner visits. **Expected:** 1–3 patient inquiries/month that mention "vi su tarjeta en [SPA]".

**Why this works:** A patient at a spa who picks up a dental card and calls = warmest possible lead. They're already in a self-care mindset.

## 7. Instagram reel production system (1/week, indefinitely)

**Pattern source:** Zack Chug (3K → 200K IG followers in 9 months via dental student content). Dr. Mike Jin founded his brand on case-study reels. Dr. Mike Dostal posts 5–7 reels/week of Q&A content. The common thread: **1 reel/week, never miss, 6+ months minimum**.

**Why Gaby is uniquely positioned:**

The strategy repo has **30 reels scripts pre-armed** at `dentist/06_MARKETING/reels-scripts/30-reels-scripts.md`. The scripts EXIST. **The execution is what matters.**

**Recommendation:** Don't try to produce 4 reels/week like the pros. Start with **1 reel/week from 1 case/week**, posted Sunday at 7 pm (Asunción time). Run for 26 weeks. Compound.

**Effort:** 1h recording + 1h editing = 2h/week. **Expected over 26 weeks:**

- 26 reels published
- ~10–50K impressions per reel (low) → average
- 200–800 new IG followers
- 5–15 dental inquiries directly attributable
- Compounding content library for year 2

**This is the single highest-ROI long-tail play** for the patient-acquisition playbook.

## 8. Pro-bono treatment for press coverage

**Pattern source:** Common pattern in US/EU for emerging specialists seeking press coverage (the "1 patient case for press" pattern). Examples: a refugee dental case, a domestic-violence survivor rehabilitation, a cleft palate for a child.

**What Gaby does:**

1. Identify 1 patient per quarter where the case has narrative weight (refugee, abuse survivor, low-income family, abandoned-by-other-clinics).
2. Discount or pro-bono the case at 50–100% off.
3. Pitch the case to ABC Color / La Nación / Última Hora as a profile piece ("Dra. González Pane rehabilita la sonrisa de [paciente] tras [circunstancia]").
4. Each press hit = 50–200K readers + local name recognition.

**Cost:** Gs 5–10M per case (deep discount or no-bill). **Expected per hit:** 10–30 new patient inquiries in the following month.

**Why I'm cautious:** Brand risk if the pro-bono is seen as "marketing stunt." Must be genuine and selected by Gaby on her own ethical framework.

**Verdict:** Skip for Y1 (too risky given no brand cushion). **Reconsider at Y2** when Ometz has 100+ reviews and brand is solid.

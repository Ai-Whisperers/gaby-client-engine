# §3 — PAID DIGITAL ADVERTISING (Meta + Google)

> Higher cost per acquisition but scales. Best used **after** foundation + word-of-mouth are working.

### 3.1 — Meta Ads (Instagram + Facebook) — interest-targeted, NOT scraped
- **Setup guide:** `dentist/06_MARKETING/meta-ads-playbook.md`.
- **First campaign (month 1, $100/mo):** Single image/video, "Limpieza dental con evaluación conservadora", CTA "Enviar mensaje WhatsApp", audience = Asunción + Luque + San Lorenzo + Fernando + 25-65 años + Intereses: "Odontología", "Implantes dentales", "Estética dental", "Blanqueamiento dental".
- **Month 2-3:** $200-300/mo. Add Lookalike-1% from page followers (after first 50 followers).
- **Month 4+:** $300-500/mo. Layer in "second-opinion" angle + retargeting site visitors.
- **Expected results:** 30-90 leads/month; CPL $3-15 in PY per dentist benchmarks (similar to Spain CAC 40-100 € for general dental).
- **Why not scraper:** IG follower scraping is ToS-banned + privacy law risk. Meta's own interest targeting is the legal equivalent.

### 3.2 — Google Ads (search-only, small budget)
- **What:** Bid on "dentista Mburucuyá", "segunda opinión dental Paraguay", "rehabilitación oral Asunción".
- **Why:** High intent. Visitors who search these terms are 5-10× more likely to book than display/IG visitors.
- **Cost:** $50-100/month (better targeting of intent → lower CPL).
- **First step:** Google Ads account setup; link to GBP.

### 3.3 — Lookalike-1% audience from email list or page followers
- **What:** Once Gaby has 100+ page followers OR 200+ emails, run Lookalike-1% — Meta's algorithm finds users most similar to them.
- **Why:** Lookalike converts 2-3× higher than interest-only targeting.
- **Trigger:** Start month 2 onwards, when data is sufficient.

### 3.4 — Retargeting visitors who didn't convert
- **What:** Pixel-installed visitors see Ometz ads on Meta for 7-30 days.
- **Why:** Conversion rate 3-5× higher than cold audience.
- **Cost:** Cheap; small budget ($30-50/mo) reaches most of them.

### 3.5 — Paid amplification of best organic content
- **What:** Each month, take the 1 reel/blog that performed best organically ($50-100 boost).
- **Why:** Already-validated content gets higher ROAS than net-new creative.
